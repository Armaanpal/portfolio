What This Was Supposed to Communicate

We were trying to communicate new musical artists to listen to for people who are tired of listening to the same artists.

Who My Audience Was

This app was created for people who want to start listening to music. Anton and I felt that most people are getting tired of the music they normally listen to.

One Design Choice I Made on Purpose

I chose to make the navigation different for each genre because I wanted my audience to like the design I chose to put in and feel like the website was legit and accurate.

Why That Design Choice Helped the Audience

This choice helped my audience because we made the navigation really nice, which made it easier for them to navigate through the page and made it easier for them to use the webpage.

One Thing I Would Improve

One thing I would improve is to add more information about the artists/add more artists. I would do this by adding a description of the artists, and I would add more artists.
